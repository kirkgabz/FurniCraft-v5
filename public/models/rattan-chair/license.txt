The source is credited in the studio's side panel via StudioModel.credit.

scene.glb is not the file as downloaded. It was converted from the supplied
binary FBX: vertices welded (810,180 -> 194,281, no change to the silhouette),
materials converted to physically based ones named "frame" and "weave", and the
whole chair turned a quarter turn about Y so its width runs along X, which is
the axis the studio's rig resizes.
